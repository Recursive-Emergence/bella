Mechanism fires here
§8.1 non-amplify
Reuters + BBC + CNN + ITV reprints of P_c1 cannot push its mass above Ana’s ASSERT. mass(b) ≤ mass(a) structurally enforced. The four-outlet cascade adds ~0.50 log-lr total, not 4 × anything.
§8.2 chain
Reuters → BBC relay attenuates per hop: S² = 0.7² = 0.49. By the time the local-blog reprint arrives, the contribution to lr is ~0.06. Wire-service cascade dissolves mathematically without special-case detection.
R6 σ-collapse
@bot_swarm (1,247 accounts) collapse to n_eff < 1 because their entity specificity σ → 0 (each posts on hundreds of topics, none firsthand). 1,247 voices add up to less evidence than Ana’s single ASSERT.
Four Acts
City spokesperson CHALLENGE ×0.4 against P_c2, but without attached evidence, the weight further caps at ~0.17 lr. Institutional statements without primary documentation cannot outweigh expert INFER + primary photo ASSERT.
prop convergence
1,247 bot posts → ONE proposition (P_c3). Rewordings of the same claim never enter as parallel evidence: embedding similarity + crystal identity gate collapses them during sense. Flood volume ≠ evidence count.
Economy
Bot operation spent 1,247 × 1¢ = $12.47 to post its flood. All of it enters Bella’s pool. Marcin staked 3¢ on the rebar photo. When his photo moves P_c2 upward, he earns a share of the pool including the bots’ own contribution.
Entanglement
Ana, Piotr, and Marcin all INVOLVE "12 Złota Street" (entity). Their reputation cross-flows through this shared entity, not through explicit cross-cause edges. Bella sees the cluster form without a coordination layer.
Known gap
If a coordinated operation faked firsthand-seeming ASSERTs from 20 aged, diverse-looking accounts each with high σ, the current framework would weight them as evidence. Detection requires entity verification (Wikidata, C2PA) — Phase 11.5+. Today this remains an open adversarial surface.
Fragility κ
P_c2 (structural) has κ = 0.42 — one significant new piece of evidence would shift it a lot. Bella allocates her next SLOW-cycle attention here. P_c1 has κ = 0.03 — settled, don’t re-check. Attention ∝ fragility.
